The following is a outline of what industry tracker will consist of

-------------
This will be intially designed with quantum computing in mind. I will also attempt to make the components modular

# Components
1. News Tracker
    - Database of news sources
    - Daily (Weekly) Search & Report
2. Job Tracker
    - Will scour job boards weekly
    - Compare with company and connection database
    - Job evaluator
3. Company database
    - Private labs
    - National labs
    
4. Grad school database
    - List of graduate schools with program
    - List of labs at said schools
        - List of PIs
            - PI's reputation and facts
        - List of grad students and postdocs in each lab
        - List of research topics
        - List of industry connections
5. Connections database
    - List of people I know in the industry or industry-adjacent    
6. Personal database (to know me)
    - Resume
    - Transcript
        - Will look
    - Books read
    - Graduation date/plans
    - Applications sent   
7. Front end dashboards
    - Able to pick and choose which industry and which features to set up with
