## Motivation
It can be difficult to keep up with all of your interests. Most of your time is consumed with work or school. The rest of the world doesn't wait. To stay informed and ready to take action on your interests is often overwhelming, if not impossible timewise. Something has to give. Either work/school suffers or you give up on your interests.
- News is too:
  - Frequent: it's impossible to consume all of it
  - Siloed: so many sources; where to look?
  - Perspective biased: How can I unbias what I ingest?
  - Uncorrelated: How can I relate different articles to each other?
  - Present biased: How does recent news change or add onto what came before it?
- The job search is:
  - Intimidating
  - Siloed
  - Saturated
  - Time-sensitive
  - Uninformative
- Networking can be:
  - Unproductive
  - Intimidating
  - Time-consuming

There has to be a better solution. Information should be centralized and easy to understand. There are too many jobs to apply to, too many labs to work in, too many people to meet. How can we prioritize? On our own, we can't.

We can fix all of this with a single, centralized platform. Customized to you and your interests. All your news in one place. Jobs, applications in one place. Graduate school. Networking. All within a platform that knows you and your interests. Career Hub.

## Use Case
Use it casually for the most part, but as it gets more information, it should become more and more helpful. Use intensely as needed.

## Market
- College students are the most obvious market. They have the most options, but also the most distractions. Days blend together, weekends aren't real. Extracurriculars are demanding. Social life is exhausting. Building the rest of your life on top of that? Hard. Almost impossible.
- High school students might use it too. Not for jobs, but for college, extracurriculars, and to stay up to date with the world.
- Grad students.
- Working people often are in what is essentially a training program for two years post-college. It's intensive and exhausting. Staying up to date often doesn't seem worth the time cost. You need to sleep, date, stay fit, and see friends. How could you find new opportunities, cool new things?
